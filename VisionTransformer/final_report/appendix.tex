\chapter{The Two Implementations of ViT-B and Run-to-Run Variation}
\label{app:impl}

Every ViT-B LoRA configuration was trained with the HuggingFace and PEFT libraries (\emph{library}) and with our own
implementation of the model and of LoRA (\emph{scratch}), from identical initial weights and with the same seeds.
Table~\ref{tab:impl} compares them. Over the seven configurations and 35 seed pairs, the mean accuracies differ by at
most 0.08~pp and single seeds by at most 0.26~pp; peak memory differs by at most 0.003~GiB; and the stopping epoch is
the same in 32 of the 35 pairs. The numbers of trainable parameters are identical. The two implementations can
therefore be used interchangeably for the results of this report. (In the earlier runs of full fine-tuning at
learning rate $10^{-4}$, the scratch implementation was 0.56~pp lower, in 4 of 5 seeds; this rate is not used as a
reference.)

\section*{Run-to-run variation on the GPU}
\label{app:variation}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Run-to-run variation on the GPU}

Attention LoRA on all blocks of ViT-B was trained in two separate sets of runs with the same settings and seeds
(Table~\ref{tab:repeat}). Memory and the number of trainable parameters are the same, and 9 of the 10 runs stopped
at the same epoch. Accuracy differs by 0.04--0.15~pp per seed, and by 0.36~pp in one run. Two ViT-B runs with the
same seed are not bit-identical on the GPU, and a small difference can change which epoch early stopping keeps: in
the run that differs by 0.36~pp, one epoch improved the validation loss by 0.0019 in the first run, just above the
required 0.001, and by 0.0001 in the second. A difference smaller than about 0.4~pp between two single ViT-B runs
should therefore not be interpreted on its own; we rely on paired differences over several seeds and on their
signs. Means over seeds are more stable but not exact: the two sets differ by 0.11 and 0.02~pp, and the repeated set
of Section~\ref{sec:acc} (three library runs, seeds 42, 123, 456: 91.19, 91.44 and 91.20\%) is 0.19~pp above the
second set of Table~\ref{tab:repeat} on the same seeds.
